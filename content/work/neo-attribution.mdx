## Overview

Neo Financial is a Calgary fintech offering digital banking, credit, savings and rewards as an alternative to Canada's big banks. Growth depended on paid acquisition, which raises the question every growth team ends up asking: which of these channels is actually producing customers, and what are they worth?

This work built the data layer that answers it, then sent the answer back into the tools the marketing team already worked in.

## Problem

Spend ran across Google, Meta, TikTok, Spotify and affiliate partners. Each platform reported its own results, and there was no shared view of what a customer cost to acquire or what they were worth once they arrived.

## My role

I led a team of four analysts and owned their output, performance and development. I also ran marketing analytics, data strategy and technology partnerships, including the marketing technology budget.

## Solution

- **A marketing analytics data mart** covering channel attribution and paid media performance across all five channels.
- **CAC and LTV models** that put acquisition cost next to lifetime value, used to guide budget allocation and judge return on spend.
- **Databricks and dbt** for the modelling, with **GitHub** for review, so attribution logic changed through pull requests instead of spreadsheet edits.
- **Hightouch** to sync modelled data back into marketing tools, so the insight showed up where decisions were made.
- **Vendor discipline.** I reviewed martech contracts and tools against actual usage to guide what we kept paying for.
- A move to **Snowflake** late in my time there.
