## Problem

Neo spent across search, social, video, audio, affiliates and more. Each platform reported its own results. There was no shared view of what a customer, or each product, cost to acquire.

## Role

As Director, Marketing Operations & Analytics, I led a team of analysts. I also owned martech partnerships and the martech budget.

## Solution

- **UTMs everywhere.** We embedded UTM tracking in every campaign link, so each new customer traced back to a channel.
- **One attribution view.** I directed a marketing analytics data mart in Databricks and dbt, run by Airflow, across every paid channel.
- **CAC and PAC.** We measured customer acquisition cost, and product acquisition cost for each product, like credit and bank accounts. With LTV models, that showed which spend paid back.
- **Reporting people used.** Reporting showed what each channel cost and returned. Hightouch synced the results back into marketing tools.
- **Vendor discipline.** I reviewed vendor contracts against actual usage before each renewal.
