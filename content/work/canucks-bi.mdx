## Overview

Canucks Sports & Entertainment runs the Vancouver Canucks (NHL), the Abbotsford Canucks (AHL), the Vancouver Warriors (NLL) and Rogers Arena. That's a lot of products sold to the same fans: memberships, single games, suites and partnerships.

I spent four years there and worked up from the front line. I started as an Account Executive managing more than 800 memberships worth over $2M. Then I moved into business intelligence as an analyst, and finished as the manager who built the BI function.

## Problem

Revenue decisions on pricing, forecasting and memberships needed better data than the organization had. Fan and sales data was split across ticketing, marketing tools and a HubSpot CRM, and there was no dedicated team or warehouse to bring it together.

## My role

- **Business Analyst, then Manager, Business Intelligence** (Jul 2021 to Dec 2023).
- Built and led a BI team of five to six at its peak, from the ground up. I did the hiring, coaching, performance management and development.
- Owned the CRM migration end to end: requirements, vendor negotiation, contract terms and rollout.
- Managed the vendor relationship with StellarAlgo, the fan data platform, years before I joined StellarAlgo.

## Solution

- **Revenue analysis.** Pricing, forecasting and membership revenue analysis that business leadership used to make decisions.
- **A data warehouse on Microsoft Fabric.** Built the organization's warehouse and reporting layer, working directly with Microsoft as an early customer from preview into general availability.
- **Automated reporting.** Pulled ticketing, pricing and campaign data together and automated recurring reports with SQL, Python and Power BI.
- **HubSpot to Salesforce.** First ran HubSpot for the Canucks and Warriors (requirements, configuration, pipeline). Then led the move to Salesforce across sales and marketing, rolled out department by department.

## Outcome

Revenue grew **15%** and then **11%** in consecutive seasons, from **$66M to $84M**, with the BI team's pricing, forecasting and membership analysis behind the decisions. The organization finished with a team, a warehouse and a CRM it didn't have when I started.
