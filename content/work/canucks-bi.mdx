## Problem

The Canucks, Abbotsford Canucks, Vancouver Warriors and Rogers Arena all sell to the same fans. Pricing and membership decisions needed better data than the organization had. There was no BI team or warehouse to bring it together.

## Role

I moved from Account Executive to Business Analyst to Manager, Business Intelligence over four years. As manager, I built the BI team and owned the CRM migration.

## Solution

- I built a BI team of five to six from the ground up, from hiring to development.
- We ran the pricing, forecasting and membership revenue analysis that business leadership used.
- I built the data warehouse and reporting layer on Microsoft Fabric, as an early customer from preview to launch.
- We automated recurring reports on ticketing, pricing and campaigns with SQL, Python and Power BI.
- I led the move from HubSpot to Salesforce: requirements, vendor negotiation, contract terms and a phased rollout.
