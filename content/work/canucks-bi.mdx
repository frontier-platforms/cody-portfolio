## Problem

The Canucks, Abbotsford Canucks, Vancouver Warriors and Rogers Arena all sell to the same fans. Too much analysis was manual, so many revenue calls ran on instinct. Results on the ice are out of anyone's control, so everything else has to be run well.

## Role

I moved from Account Executive to Business Analyst to Manager, Business Intelligence over four years. As manager, I led the BI team and owned the CRM migration.

## Solution

We made BI part of how the business decided. It guided who to call and when, how to price, and how to react when things went badly.

- **Automation.** We automated recurring reports on ticketing, pricing and campaigns with SQL, Python and Power BI. Analysts spent their time on decisions, not on rebuilding spreadsheets.
- **Machine learning.** Models helped decide who to call and when, and made pricing and sales forecasts more accurate.
- **One warehouse.** I built the warehouse and reporting layer on Microsoft Fabric, as an early customer from preview to launch.
- **A CRM sales could trust.** I led the move from HubSpot to Salesforce: requirements, vendor negotiation and a phased rollout.
- **Early warning.** When a season turned, we saw it in the numbers early and adjusted pricing, offers and outreach.
