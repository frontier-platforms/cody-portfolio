## Overview

Valve is software for plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs, roofers, landscapers and general contractors. It covers the work of running a small trades business: jobs, quotes, invoices, scheduling, customers and getting paid. It's meant to be the simple tool a five-person crew will actually use, not an enterprise system they'll never finish setting up.

I'm co-founding it as a side venture and writing a lot of the code myself. It's where I stay sharp on the full modern stack.

## Problem

Small trades businesses tend to run on a mix of paper, texts, spreadsheets and an accounting package. Quotes get lost, invoices go out late, and the owner is the only person who knows the schedule. The existing software tends to be built for much bigger companies.

## Solution

- **Next.js and TypeScript** on **Vercel** for the web app.
- **Supabase** for Postgres, auth, storage and row-level security, so each business only ever sees its own data.
- **React Native** for the crew's field app.
- **Stripe Connect** so each business takes payments directly from its own customers.
- **Claude API** for an assistant that knows the business's own jobs, customers and invoices.
- An **MCP server** that lets AI assistants read and act on Valve data through a defined, permissioned interface instead of screen scraping.
- Quotes with e-signature, invoices, crew scheduling, SMS and email in one inbox, and reports on revenue and job types.
