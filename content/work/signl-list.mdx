## Problem

Most to-do apps make it easy to add tasks and hard to choose. The list grows, the important work hides in it, and unfinished tasks turn into guilt.

## Role

I built Signl List as a side project. I own the product, the design and the code.

## Solution

- Each morning you pick your priorities for the day, three by default. Those are your signal.
- Insights shows signal versus noise: how much of your time went to priorities.
- Unfinished tasks carry forward. After three moves, the app asks if they still matter.
- Routines live apart from to-dos, so a missed habit never clutters today's list.
- Recovery days and streaks reward rest instead of punishing it.

It's an Expo and React Native app, built and shipped with EAS. Data stays on the phone, with no account needed. See the [product page](https://www.frontier-platforms.com/signl-list).
