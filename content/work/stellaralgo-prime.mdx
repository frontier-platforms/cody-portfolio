## Problem

Client data at StellarAlgo could be up to 7 days old, since a key process ran weekly by hand. For a sports team, a week is a homestand. Data ownership was also spread across teams, with no clear service levels.

## Role

I led PRIME from planning through launch as Director, Product Innovation & Analytics. I coordinated Product, Data Engineering and Customer Success with a product pod of four.

## Solution

- I automated the weekly manual process into a daily run.
- I rebuilt the models in Snowflake and dbt with bronze, silver and gold layers. More than 100 models are tested and documented.
- I wrote a data contract that set ownership, service levels and escalation paths for every team.
- I shipped an internal Customer Success bot on the modelled data. I took Stellar Insights, a client-facing analytics product, to dev stage.
- I'm now leading the move of analytics workloads from AWS Athena to Snowflake.
