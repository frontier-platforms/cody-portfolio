## Overview

StellarAlgo is a Calgary software company that helps professional sports and entertainment organizations unify their fan data and grow ticket, sponsorship and partnership revenue. Its value depends on clients trusting the data enough to act on it.

PRIME is the centralized analytics platform those clients use. I led it from planning through launch. Once the data was fresh and modelled consistently, it became possible to put AI on top without the AI repeating bad numbers.

## Problem

A key process ran manually once a week, so client data could be up to **7 days** old. For a sports team, a week is a homestand. Ownership of the data was also spread across teams, without clear service levels or a clear path when something broke.

## My role

- **Director, Product Innovation & Analytics** (Jan 2025 to Jan 2026), then **Director, Product** (Jan 2026 to present).
- Led PRIME from planning through launch, coordinating Product, Data Engineering and Customer Success, with a product pod of four.
- Now own the product roadmap and annual budget for all analytics products.

## Solution

- **Automation.** Turned the manual weekly process into an automated daily one. Data freshness went from 7 days to under 12 hours, and the team got its time back for higher-value work.
- **Snowflake and dbt, in a medallion architecture.** Raw data lands untouched, gets cleaned and conformed once, then gets shaped into business-ready models. More than 100 dbt models are version controlled, tested and documented.
- **A data contract.** A formal agreement across departments on ownership, service levels and accountability, so every team knows its responsibilities and escalation path.
- **An AI layer on the modelled data:**
  - An **internal Customer Success bot** that answers questions about client data, shipped and in use.
  - **Stellar Insights**, a customer-facing natural-language analytics product. I ran discovery through stakeholder interviews across sports clients, turned the findings into recommendations for leadership, and took it to dev stage.
- **What's next.** I'm now leading the migration of analytics workloads from AWS Athena to Snowflake, owning the planning, vendor negotiation, budget and delivery.
